# Repeaty

A skill-learning platform where learners maintain streaks by recording daily practice sessions — inspired by Duolingo's engagement model.

## What is Repeaty?

Repeaty is built on a simple idea: the best way to learn a skill is to practice it consistently, every single day. Inspired by Duolingo's streak mechanism, Repeaty holds learners accountable by having them record a short video of their practice session each day to keep their streak alive.

## How It Works

1. **Pick a skill** — anything you want to get better at
2. **Practice daily** — record a short video of your session through the app
3. **Keep your streak** — miss a day and your streak resets
4. **Track your progress** — watch your consistency and improvement over time

## Motivation

Consistency beats intensity. Most people don't fail to learn a skill because they lack talent — they fail because they stop showing up. Repeaty is designed to make showing up the default.
